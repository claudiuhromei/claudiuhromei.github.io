*ExtremITA at EVALITA 2023: Multi-Task Sustainable Scaling to Large Language Models at its Extreme* received the Best Paper Award at EVALITA 2023 (Parma, Italy). A single multi-task LLM took part in all the tasks of the campaign and won 41% of the tracks.
