Our paper *MM-IGLU: Multi-Modal Interactive Grounded Language Understanding* is in the proceedings of LREC-COLING 2024 (Torino, Italy). It introduces a benchmark and a model for agents that build structures in Minecraft-like worlds and ask for clarifications when a command is ambiguous.
