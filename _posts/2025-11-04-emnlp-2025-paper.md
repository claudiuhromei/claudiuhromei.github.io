Our paper *Grounded Semantic Role Labelling from Synthetic Multimodal Data for Situated Robot Commands* is in the proceedings of EMNLP 2025 (Suzhou, China). It shows how diffusion models and LLMs can generate thousands of synthetic image–command pairs to train robots that understand situated commands.
