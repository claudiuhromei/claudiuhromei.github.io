## Professional experience

### Senior AI Scientist (Finance) · BIP (Business Integration Partners)

*May 2025 – present*

I lead the architectural design and deployment of enterprise-grade, agentic AI solutions for major institutions.

- **Complex multi-agent systems:** architected an orchestration system that automates the generation of

  paradigms into scalable production systems.

*November 2020 – April 2025*

  understanding modules into a robotic platform for patient rehabilitation, enhancing its cognitive
