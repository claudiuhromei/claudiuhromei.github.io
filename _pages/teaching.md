## Corporate AI training

*2025 – present*

I design and deliver executive and hands-on AI adoption programmes for Tier-1 banking, public sector,
broadcasting and transport institutions. The courses cover AI governance, AI for procurement, Copilot
integration, agentic workflows and prompt engineering, with the goal of bringing AI into everyday business
processes.

## Thesis supervision

*2021 – present*

I have co-supervised more than 20 B.Sc. and M.Sc. theses on context-aware robotic interfaces, multimodal LLMs
and prompt engineering, mentoring students through the full research lifecycle. Several of these projects led
to joint publications at top-tier conferences such as ACL and EMNLP.
