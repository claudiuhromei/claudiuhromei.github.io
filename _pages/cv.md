## Summary

Senior AI Scientist and Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence, with over five years of experience bridging
cutting-edge research (EMNLP, ACL) and enterprise-grade AI solutions. My expertise covers generative AI
agents, multimodal Large Language Models and grounded interaction. I currently lead the development of complex
multi-agent systems for the finance sector using the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Azure OpenAI, and I teach
enterprise AI adoption and agentic AI to Tier-1 public and private organisations.
